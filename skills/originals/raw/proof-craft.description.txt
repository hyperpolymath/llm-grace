Use when developing, verifying, auditing, refactoring, or presenting mathematical proofs in any register — informal paper-grade proofs, machine-checked proofs in Lean 4, Coq/Rocq, Isabelle/HOL, or Agda, or solver queries (Z3, cvc5, Vampire, E; SMT-LIB, TPTP).
